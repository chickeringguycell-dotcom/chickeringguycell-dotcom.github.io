Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to AI Notebook Pro and all associated productivity applications published by our team.

1. Data Collection & Usage

AI Notebook Pro is designed with user privacy as a primary principle. Your notes, code snippets, calculation formulas, and attached media are stored locally on your device.

2. Camera & Photo Attachments

When you take photos or attach images to your notes, the images are stored locally in your device's private storage. No photos are transmitted, sold, or shared with third parties.

3. Microphone & Voice Dictation

When you use the speech-to-text dictation feature, audio is processed locally using your device's operating system speech recognition engine solely to transcribe your spoken words into text notes.

4. AI Features & APIs

When you optionally request AI drafting, summarization, or coding help, the specific prompt text is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google Gemini API servers solely to generate the requested content. No user data is retained for marketing purposes.

5. Strict Non-Sharing Policy

No Third-Party Data Selling:
We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize user personal data under any circumstances.
